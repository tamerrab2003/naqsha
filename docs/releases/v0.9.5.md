# Naqsha 0.9.5: 17 languages, Arabic and right-to-left

Naqsha now speaks your language. This release covers everything since 0.9.2.

![The same order flowchart in Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese and Thai](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tamerrab2003/naqsha/main/docs/images/languages-light.png)

## The app in 17 languages
- English, العربية, Français, Español, Português, हिन्दी, فارسی, اردو, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, ไทย, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, Filipino and Kiswahili. This covers the menus, ribbon, panels, dialogs, template gallery, shape and symbol names, Pro and licence screens.
- Naqsha starts in your system's language. Change it with the **globe button** in the title bar, or add `?lang=` to the browser version's address (`nixpx.com/naqsha/app?lang=ar`).
- **Arabic, Persian and Urdu mirror the window:** shapes on the right, the format pane on the left, and the ribbon, tabs and status bar right to left.
- New shapes get their default text in your language.

## Text in every script (free)
- **Arabic, Persian and Urdu** on the canvas: joined letters, lam-alef ligatures, harakat, and correct order when Arabic is mixed with Latin tags and numbers (`المضخة P-101`). Wrapped lines start where they should. UML and ER compartments align Arabic to the right.
- **Hindi (Devanagari), Thai, Chinese and Japanese** are drawn with built-in Noto fonts, so they look the same on every computer. Chinese and Japanese lines wrap between characters.
- **Exports** (SVG, PNG, PDF) contain the real text: selectable, searchable, in the right order and the same fonts as the canvas. This fixes reversed Arabic, doubled Hindi and Thai letters, Hindi vowel signs being overlapped, and random fallback fonts in PNG and PDF.
- The browser version downloads the Chinese/Japanese font (4 MB) only the first time a drawing needs it.

## Office and ONLYOFFICE
- The ONLYOFFICE plugin follows the editor's light or dark theme, and was tightened after ONLYOFFICE's marketplace review.

## Known limits
- Text the app generates inside drawings (legends, title blocks, line-balance charts, Excel schedules) and the sample content of templates are still in English.
- Thai lines break between characters, not between words. Urdu is shown in Naskh, not Nastaliq. Rare Chinese and Japanese characters outside the everyday set (GB 2312, Big5 level 1, JIS level 1) show as boxes.
- The translations are new: if something reads wrong in your language, please [open an issue](https://github.com/tamerrab2003/naqsha/issues).

**Downloads** are below: macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows, Linux (.deb and .rpm), the browser version (`naqsha-0.9.5-web.zip`, also live at [nixpx.com/naqsha/app](https://nixpx.com/naqsha/app)) and the ONLYOFFICE plugin.
