# Naqsha 0.9.7: powder handling and food & beverage symbols

Two new Naqsha Pro symbol groups for process engineers, and three new basic shapes.

## Bulk Solids & Powder (Pro)
24 P&ID symbols for pneumatic and mechanical conveying of powders: sack tip station, big bag
unloader (FIBC), pick-up and feed hoppers, silo with vent filter, weigh hopper on load cells,
vacuum receiver, vacuum pump / exhauster, dust filter, pressure conveying vessel, rotary, diverter
and slide gate valves, flexible connection, screw conveyor, loss-in-weight and volumetric feeders,
flat deck sieve, centrifugal sifter, in-line magnet, lump breaker, batch and continuous mixers and
a vibrator. Each one numbers its own tags (RV-101, LIW-101, …).

## Food & Beverage (Pro)
27 symbols for food processing: chillers and freezers (tumble, spiral, IQF, hydro-cooler), blancher,
HTST pasteurizer, fryer, roaster, jacketed kettle, retort, spray dryer, evaporator, ribbon blender,
high-shear mixer, homogenizer, separator, optical sorter, washer, grinder, extruder, forming
machine, dicer, depositor, aseptic filler, tray sealer and CIP skid.

## Shapes
- Semicircle, heart and crescent in Basic shapes; they also open from and save to draw.io.

Both new groups and the new shapes are named in all 17 languages.

## Fixes
- `naqsha --export` no longer crashes in debug builds.
