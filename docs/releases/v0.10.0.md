# Naqsha 0.10.0: diagrams that tell a story

Turn a diagram into a step-by-step explainer, present it full screen, or export it as a video
for TikTok, Reels, YouTube or LinkedIn.

## Story
Each page can have a story: a list of steps, each with a caption. In the new **Story** tab beside
Format, choose what happens to the selected shapes and connectors at each step:
- **Appear** / **Disappear**: build the diagram up piece by piece.
- **Run** / **Stop** their animation: a connector flows, a shape pulses.
- **Alarm**: they shake, then glow red.
- **Send**: a dot travels along a connector, or along every connector leaving a shape.
- **Set Text**: a label changes; numbers count up to their new value (0 → 1,248 passed).
- **Spotlight** (optionally dimming everything else) and **Frame** (the view zooms to them).

New items pop in, rise or fade, one after another, and connectors draw themselves from start to
end. **Play Step** shows a step's motion right on the canvas.

## Present
**View › Present** (F5, or Shift+F5 from the current page) plays the stories full screen. The view
glides to each step and the caption shows along the bottom. Use the arrow keys, Space, a
presentation clicker or a click to move. After a page's last step the next page comes on, and
Esc ends the presentation.

## Video (desktop)
**File › Export › Video** turns a page's story into an MP4: tall 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and
Shorts; wide 16:9 for YouTube and LinkedIn; or square. It can add a title across the top, and it
shows a small "Made with Naqsha" in the corner. From the command line:
`naqsha --export diagram.nqs out.mp4 --wide --title "…"`.

With **Naqsha Pro**, videos can also:
- carry your logo and your own colour;
- leave out the "Made with Naqsha" mark;
- play every page's story, one page after another, in one video;
- be made at 60 frames a second or in 4K.

The CI/CD Pipeline template now comes with a ready-made story to try all of this on.

## Also new
- **Insert › Template**: add any gallery template to the open diagram, as new pages or onto
  this page.
- **Copy as Image**: copy the selection, or the whole page, to the clipboard as a picture
  (free version included).

Everything new is translated into all 17 languages.

## Fixes
- Text and symbol labels now fade with their shape's opacity, and connector labels fade with
  their connector.
