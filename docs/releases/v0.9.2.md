# Naqsha 0.9.2: production lines, moving diagrams and Node-RED

This release brings together everything since 0.7.

![A bottling line with product flowing and the bottleneck glowing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tamerrab2003/naqsha/main/docs/images/production-line.gif)

## Moving diagrams (free)
- Connectors can **flow** (dashes running toward the arrow) or carry **moving dots**; shapes can **glow, pulse, blink, spin or shake**. Set it in the Format pane › Animation, with speed and colour.
- **View › Play / Pause.**
- **File › GIF** exports one loop as an animated GIF; **SVG** export keeps the motion (CSS, respects reduced motion). PNG and PDF show the first frame.
- `naqsha --export line.nqs line.gif` from the command line.

## Node-RED (free)
- **Insert › Node-RED**, open a `flows.json`, or simply paste a copied flow: tabs become pages, subflows open from the nodes that use them, groups, link wires and configuration nodes come along.
- **Live views:** the new [`node-red-contrib-naqsha`](https://github.com/tamerrab2003/naqsha/tree/main/nodered) add-on serves a Naqsha drawing at `/naqsha/<path>` on your Node-RED and puts values on it: badges with units, running and stopped states that start and stop animations, red alarms. Monitoring only.

## Naqsha Pro
- **Production lines** for food, packaging and pharma: 31 line-equipment symbols and 13 value stream map icons.
- **Line Balance:** takt time, bottleneck, line output and efficiency from each machine's cycle time and uptime, a marker on the bottleneck and a balance chart page. **VSM Timeline** builds data boxes and the lead-time ladder.
- **IoT & Automation:** 26 symbols (sensors, PLC, RTU, gateways, MQTT broker, flow engine, time-series database, cloud, dashboards).
- **Nine new templates:** bottling line, pharma tablet line, snack packaging line, U-shaped assembly cell, current-state VSM, IIoT monitoring architecture, Purdue model, pump station telemetry, LoRaWAN cold chain.
- Pro is now on sale: $8 a month or $79 a year, or $129 for a lifetime licence. Every edition starts with a 14-day free trial. Licences bought from 0.9.1 on need 0.9.1 or later.

## Office and ONLYOFFICE
- Microsoft Office add-in: the Naqsha pane opens with files that hold a diagram, Edit follows the selection, and **Save (⌘S)** in the editor puts the diagram back into the document.
- ONLYOFFICE plugin: submitted to the ONLYOFFICE marketplace; meanwhile install it from this release (`naqsha-onlyoffice.plugin`).

## Look and feel
- A lighter, crisper grid, one physical pixel wide as in Visio.
- A redrawn Person shape, and smoother curves at every zoom.
- Connectors leaving the bottom of an equipment symbol start below its tag instead of crossing it.
